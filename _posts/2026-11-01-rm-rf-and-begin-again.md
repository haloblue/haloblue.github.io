The old version of this site sat untouched for years. It had one paragraph admitting I no longer write HTML as fast as I think, and a folder of Google Web Designer exports from an era I'd rather not explain. Both are gone now.

This version is simpler: a place to write things down. Security and systems, mostly — I've spent three decades in that world — but I reserve the right to wander.

In the spirit of not deleting one's own words, here's what the old page said, salvaged intact:

> Borders are a political concept that should allow for permeability and not lines to define cultures. To what end though? While the centralized model of the Nation State wanes, what will be there to replace it? A global entity? Doubtful, as that is not a viable option given current technological states and population size and geographic dispersion.
>
> We assign weights to communication mediums by generation.

Half-finished thoughts, both of them. That's roughly what this site is for.
